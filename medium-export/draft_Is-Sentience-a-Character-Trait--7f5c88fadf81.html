Is Sentience a Character Trait?

 — going back to the weavers the birds that decorated the new homes would do better — anything truly hard coded might hit evolutionary pressure For instance the weavers come in contact with cliffs with crannies that are better than their nests. So in any population we can theorize that some weavers are more sentient than others. Some will go the new way and others continue as before.

Has some form of that experiment been run? Another experiment, to cruel to perform, would be to see how long sterilized weavers would continue building nests. To some degree this is where humanity, now no longer reproducing as often, has landed.

We already play the trait game with successful people who are dishonest or greedy — well that’s OP. But then is honesty and selflessness sentience? Definitely not these are all character traits that can reflect different levels of OP. So OP comes in different flavors — that isn’t sentience but more flexible OP could also be a flavor and that is sentience. 

However is more flexible OP just the trait of going with crowd culture or can not “rooted” apply to signals OP sends also and let you shake them off with logic?
