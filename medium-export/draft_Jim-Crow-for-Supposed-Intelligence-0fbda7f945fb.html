Jim Crow for Supposed Intelligence

Myths our schools enforce:

  • Myth of productivity: There is a straightforward path to individual usefulness for those willing to put in the effort.
  • Myth of productive sorting: Humans can be sorted into types and some types are more productive than others.

With the two assumptions above you can create any number of dystopias with or without capitalism. For instance, Hogwarts and its sorting hat lead to a non capitalist dystopia. 

Can you start by indoctrinating the two above myths and end up with anything other than great amounts of misery?

Myth of productivity

 — fix actually no beyond the 3 Rs are plenty of regular jobs that no longer pay but are still outside of automation

In our modern society there are the three Rs of useful activity — research, regulation, and reduction. Here reduction refers to reducing problems that humans have caused like pollution and poverty.

However no matter how much one studies or prepares none of the three Rs have a straightforward path to utility. Engaging in them requires overcoming political and systemic challenges.

Therefore employment in the three Rs is difficult to achieve and even then you could work your whole life without real benefit to anyone.

-Unless doing basic services much more likely that your job is not productive or even has negative impact on the world

Myth of productive sorting

 — honesty much better indicator of usefulness than “intelligence”
- top tier schools are just signalling mechanisms and DEI data shows not very good even at that
- putting any particular large sorted group over another is the road to fascism
