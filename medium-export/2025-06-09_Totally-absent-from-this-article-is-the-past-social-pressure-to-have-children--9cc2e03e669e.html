Totally absent from this article is the past social pressure to have children. In a more Malthusian world not having children would mean the quick end of your society. Plus no children would quickly lead to no army in cases where any fighting had to be done.

You can definitely use modern technology to make raising children easier and every industrialized society should do so. However, in a world of over 8 billion and reduced traditional warfare, it might be awhile before young people feel any existential threat from not having children.
