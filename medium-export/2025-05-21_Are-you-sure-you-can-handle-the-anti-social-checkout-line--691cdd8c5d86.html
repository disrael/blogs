Or god forbid maybe one the eggs you bought is cracked. What the hell is wrong with you holding up this entire line by not thoroughly checking those eggs on your time! Or maybe you brought your own bags but didn't bring enough and now the cashier is forced to ask you if you want to purchase (if you are in a state like California) another bag. Screw you and your incompetent bag choices!!!!!!
