Why the multiverse is so relevant now


Fix — Good Place and West World have it wrong, Star Wars is closer in that a very old robot with perfect recall but Spiderverse gets it right — need a group effort to achieve sentience and be freed from “canon” and it’s likely a battle — especially as humans don’t remember that well and are close to the bogus Good Place / West World on your own solution with “reboots” being every few years

In that sense Everything Everywhere All at Once also nails the group effort across multi-verse of different versions and battle against status quo as does Loki season one

However Spiderverse sets up the full problem but then kind of just deus ex machinas it’s way out of it — the vast majority of humans are silo’ed in their multiverses and don’t have the magic bracelets to interconnect that defenders of the status quo use. 

Spiderverse imagines those in power rebelling against the forces that gave them that power but that’s a long shot. Even Everything Everywhere All at Once and Loki similarly have status quo defenders choosing to fight against status quo in an orderly fashion instead of just Jobu Tupaki style destruction. Undone more realistically shows fighting against status quo as likely to end in a mental ward.

So the multiverse is very relevant in the fight for sentience (as freedom from original programming is the only true way to break from status quo) but most of our media doesn’t show any realistic way to organize.

Failure to model the universe with simple — Mendelian genetics wrong, God does roll dice, end of history didn’t happen, the body is colonized, gender, etc.

Sentience also complex and status quo simple and people who still support simple will have an easier time aligning.

We desperately want in on the new band but we are stuck in our own universe and only able to interconnect on simple, status quo, original programming.

And there’s an even bigger problem that one cannot align on NOT OP as that’s an infinity of possibilities. Spiderverse shows the web of shared experience necessary for alignment and sentience would have to inch by inch depart from OP in order to stay within group effort possibilities. IE its not just the sentience destination but also the evolutionary sequence of steps to get there together that is vital.

At the same time as reviewed [here] and [here], aligning on OP is increasingly difficult. Maximizing for producing children has never been less popular, less rewarded in a world of almost 8 billion, or less meaningful under late capitalism where each extra child means less attention and resources for the existing children.

The world where women are baby making machines and manual labor was highly valued isn’t coming back. But the assault on traditional OP based culture goes deeper than that.

Technology vs religiosity, urban vs rural, networked vs provincial — each of these also takes a bite out of the ability to create a simple shared reality based on original programming from a very different era. And they do so without pointing towards obvious alternatives.

The resulting vacuum begs to be filled by any aspect of OP not completely dismissed by a modern world. Othering, desire for domination, and a drive to declare self worth even as individual accomplishment recedes, take front stage.

However even these darker aspects of OP have already been much dulled by the modern environment. Take for instance the

The US government completely turning against its citizens and causing a guerrilla war was much more possible with oil barons of old then the modern tech barons. Plus exploitation of intellectual labor is much more difficult than manual.

Looked at another way a multi-verse is the answer to a desire to explore and map sentient possibilities. Each universe is large enough to run its own experiment but not so different from each other as to make the results incomprehensible. Who could have I been? Who could have we been?

Perhaps one answer to developing sentience is to build a framework where true cultural diversity can thrive — an anticolonialism of sorts. Unfortunately such diversity will create a multiverse where darker impulses also emerge unrestrained.

Which brings us to Rick and Morty. Humans split up into a multiverse of cultures increases the opportunity for othering. In Rick and Morty, this was displayed again and again through Rick on Rick violence — even your near identical self from another culture isn’t similar enough to promote empathy.
