This just isn't true. In any relationship one person will invariably try harder than the other - not everything can be exactly equal.

Even one person trying and the other not trying can work if it gets them through some barrier. Maybe if your friend had done more than just text; ramped up the effort when he felt it was one-sided things would have worked out.

Even a two way street is not symmetric all the time.
