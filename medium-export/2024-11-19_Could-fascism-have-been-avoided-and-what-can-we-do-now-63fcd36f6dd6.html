Could fascism have been avoided and what can we do now

Agreeing on what went long term wrong is important in case there is another chance for a different America post Trump.

Fascism : a populist political philosophy, movement, or regime that exalts nation and often race above the individual, that is associated with a centralized autocratic government headed by a dictatorial leader, and that is characterized by severe economic and social regimentation and by forcible suppression of opposition

The root cause of our last election has two main viewpoints:

Unavoidable — racism, wild west, and patriarchy was our early DNA and there was a stream of “individual freedom” lovers that led to fascism.

VS

Avoidable — fooled around and found out: if you don’t want to be fascist then Democrats should stop playing with fascism.

Below we explore each and then what we can do now.

But unlimited growth was hard coded in American DNA from the start by the willingness to use slaves and slaughter natives to achieve it.

Then there are other indicators that America is irredeemable:

  • “Southern strategy” a thing because slave heritage never goes away.
  • Bill Clinton was the first Democratic president since FDR to win two consecutive terms and that was only possible with his hard right tack.
  • The Reagan revolution was already relatively fascist. The combination of reduced government and a growing economy leads to robber baron strong men and pseudo-dictators. When Reagan said, “Fascism was really the basis of the New Deal. It was Mussolini’s success in Italy, with his government-directed economy, that led early New Dealers to say, ‘but Mussolini keeps the trains running on time.’”, he missed the central point of fascism — dictators. You don’t avoid dictators with less government; you avoid dictators with more and better government.

Fascism is built on contradictions and so historically falls apart again and again; usually in some very painful outcomes for nations that embrace it. Things will now get a lot worse for the American fascists and there is no need for sympathy.

YES fascism could have been avoided

America is a much the home to regulation and limits as it is to unlimited growth. Many major advances in anti-fascism started in America — ending monarchy, 5 day work weeks, child labor laws, Wyoming was the second with women’s suffrage (behind the Pitcairn Islands), birth control, freedom of religion, etc.

To see how fascism might have been avoided we have to understand Bill Clinton. If “third way” works then you are headed straight for facism no matter what:

Within the field of fascism studies the trope of the ‘third way’ has served as an invaluable asset in terms of granting fascism ideological autonomy and originality; and this is why it is now widely recognised as one of the core ideological components of generic fascism.6

Democrats voting for a fascism pre-cursor came before Trump

But what most people don’t know is that in 1996 — fresh off the heels of signing welfare reform, and two years after signing the “crime bill” — President Bill Clinton signed a bill that overhauled immigration enforcement in the US and laid the groundwork for the massive deportation machine that exists today.
