The Patriarchy Scam

All three legs of the white male “respect” stool are threatened - career, family, and patriarchy and the right is freaking out in a way that will hurt everyone but mostly them.

By some estimates patriarchy has been around since agriculture started over 10,000 years ago. However older patriarchy usually had women spending more time raising children and male careers like fighting, farming, or factories.

Since changing career and family outcomes is hard, the right is doubling down on patriarchy but it’s a scam.

Career

Wealth inequality and poor job prospects are the obvious candidate for new events to cause a right uprising, but the gilded age and progressive era also had equivalent economic issues.

Average life expectancy at birth, average life expectancy at 10 years old and adult height measures all trended downward during the Gilded Age, even in studies which control for factors such as demographic changes brought on by immigration.

Both periods also had rising union membership which softened the wealth inequality. However a services economy is difficult to unionize — lower skilled, smaller workplaces, and more part time. Reducing immigration and tariffs can’t change decades long manufacturing trends anytime soon.

Women in the workforce combined with inability to unionize means “respect” from career is harder for most men; unless like Putin you draft young men into a war you started.

Family

The world’s lowering birth rate means praise for raising a family is harder to achieve. Fewer children are born and acceptance of the importance of raising a family is also lower.

Autocracy does not solve anything. China and Russia have even worse issues with wealth inequality and low birth rates.

Above is already not fair to women, but if you include single mothers and limit the data to families with children then you get an even worse picture.

Could the white male respect problem be solved by returning to mostly husband income households? No because with tremendous wealth inequality the only way to restore respect is to tax the rich more — a luxury goods tax for instance. Otherwise those white males would just be single income in households with much lower status than others.

Traditional Patriarchy

At the height of patriarchy Thomas Jefferson had a shadow family with a teenage “black” girl 14 to 16 years old. White men in America considered themselves scientifically superior to other races and women.

In 1790 a census showed one third of southern households owned slaves. However 25 to 70 thousand American men died in the revolutionary war out of roughly 1.25 million men, around 4%. Plus there was no casual dating and in the 1780's men didn’t marry till mid to late twenties.

By 1780 the percentage of adult colonists who formally held membership in a church was between 10 and 30%. Less than half of Americans, 45%, now belong to a formal house of worship.

In 1890, 85% of Americans were born in the United States and currently it’s about the same. So peak patriarchy only requires economic subjugation of women and other races — not strong religious participation or low immigration.

However that leaves patriarchy as a divide with no compromise. States that allow women control of their bodies cannot easily coexist next to states that don’t.

A form of soft succession is already under way — you can force people to end slavery but you can’t force a transition from red to blue. Some humans may have been forced to adopt agriculture but there is evidence that for many it was a long process of choice.
