5 easy ways to protect our politicians from billionaire violence, now, before more happens

1. Queue up state limitarian referendums

An example of a limitarian referendum is taxing billionaires at a much higher rate, but there are many ways to go and states have the power to enact these policies within their borders. For states with no citizen-initiated ballot measures — get them.

These referendums should be constantly on the ballot anyway, but they also serve as a deterrent. Should something happen to an anti-oligarch politician the ballot will pass at the next election.

2. Attend No Kings protests

Some have complained these protests haven’t yet made specific demands. That’s okay; “the people” are just warming up.

These protests help build the community and infrastructure necessary for limitarian action. If we can keep the protests going volunteer sign up sheets and signatures for referendums will eventually be a focus.

3. Let anti-oligarch politicians appear remotely

A giant monitor with an anti-oligarch speaking to a crowd should be enough. Don’t make these very brave politicians further risk their lives to do more than that. Even if the event security is airtight there is the travel to and from the event to consider.

4. Elect more anti-oligarch politicians and encourage existing officials to take a stand

Safety in numbers.
