Even if one is not concerned at all with inequality or fairness there is still the matter of stagnation. Our society is failing to produce the conditions necessary for people to reproduce or even lead lives as rich as their parents.

Think of a broken meritocracy in terms of being unable to allocate resources toward anything of merit. All of us, even the ones at the top, are living diminished lives because medical advances, great entertainment, better infrastructure, etc. has been sidelined to keep the pretense of meritocracy going.
