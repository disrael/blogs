Is the absence of harems among super wealth men a sign of sentience?

  • Of course Genghis Khan level procreation will no longer be mathematically effective towards your genes going viral — assuming it was even for him. But OP doesn’t do math — only culture does and the super wealthy are not under any breeding in captivity morose — so is it sentience?
  • Maybe that Genghis Khan stuff got sublimated into all flirt and no fuck gratification working off OP but subverted
  • 90/300 maturity model means all relationships are about grooming
  • Of course the 300 model is very over simplified but look at 10th symphonies or artists like Picasso, Goya and Miles Davis — there is every reason to think they would have continued to grow and mature and that’s not even including well rounding
  • Exhibit A — wife in Invincible take down of most powerful man in the galaxy — but as see in baseball scene that’s with all of Earth culture at her disposal so maybe is not just her
  • Our ideas about our fake meritocracy being real come first from the idea that relationships offer some sort of meritocracy. That mates can be chosen in any sort of an evolutionary precise manner. If mates can be chosen that way then it lends credence to jobs being potentially handed out with some semblance of justification also — ignoring whether or not those jobs do anything.
  • Here we run into the idea that sexual selection = original programming. Our OP purely created for and driven by finding a mate. If that’s true then instead of relationships modifying OP we have relationships = the exercising of OP.
  • Our entire economy can then be geared toward satisfying OP’s need to rank and sort for sexual selection. Producing actual value almost becomes irrelevant if the entire economy serves as a sexual selection. While it is true that sexual selection doesn’t have to run on fake value https://www.pnas.org/post/journal-club/peacock-s-tail-and-other-flashy-ornaments-don-t-have-come-cost it’s also true that it can https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handicap_principle.
  • The problem here is that mates can and probably should (for health reasons of younger being better when producing a baby) be chosen long before there is any economic outcome. Chosen that way then the sexual selection comes purely from academia and fairly early academic success at that and then the entire rest of the economy is left as an afterthought.
  • So the exercising of OP = sexual selection results in a society where a fake economy is almost an unavoidable consequence.
  • This indicates that sentience might require removing all academic accomplishment and sorting. No fake sexual selection might lead to no fake meritocracy, fake work, etc. And it’s an application of culture => sentience.
  • However that would likely only emphasize looks and charm over hard “work” studying and so create a looks and charm economy as well. Or it might unreasonably emphasize lineage and create a pure nepotism economy and strict class based mating.
  • On the other hand nearly anything that can be ascribed to sexual selection and handicap theory can also have a cultural origin and be a step towards sentience https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00191-021-00753-4 Does academic success really lead to improved attractiveness? Or are we already pursuing a cultural detour from OP? Or is it a combination where like attracts like for both culture evolution and mate selection at the same time?
  • Even the determination of fake work is difficult as it can be with handicap theory as well. A female weaverbird chooses a mate by quality of nest but at some level of quality we detour from practicality to handicap honest signalling via suffering.
  • No matter how pleasant an economy we devise OP would still have us beginning adulthood with a romance hunger games of sorts. Since this is the first impression any young adult gets the entire rest of the economy becomes a side note — if following OP.
  • If less OP one could travel the world or do low paying work and ignore career altogether but realistically only people who reproduce will be represented in the next generation — the rebellion against OP would be short lived unless culture is strong enough to make the generational leap
  • Unsupervised dating is not that old https://www.theatlantic.com/family/archive/2020/02/modern-dating-odds-economy-apps-tinder-math/606982/ but the supervised version was just as cutthroat with dowries, first child inherits all, etc.
  • So culturally mating would seem to be the place to try for sentience — education and the economy might be knock on effects
  • https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/33613855-the-evolution-of-desire argues that desirability has a long history like taste in food and is therefore not always subject to culture — sweet tastes sweet. However cultures vary widely in how sweet or spicy they like their food and the results seem very subject to culture even for a single individual’s tastes. There are of course upper bounds on this range but still.
  • However facial beauty unlike culturally variable body fat percentage is not learned and can detected by babies. So too maybe other traits like female waist to hip ratio. As soon as anything is hard coded there is trouble as we will be pure robot to its influence — disregarding memory and culture.
